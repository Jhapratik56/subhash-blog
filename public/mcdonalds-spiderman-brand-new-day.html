What the promotion actually is

McDonalds confirmed its Spiderman Brand New Day partnership as part of a wider marketing wave that includes Sony Pictures working with roughly twelve companies worldwide. The chain shared a promotional video on Instagram showing a Happy Meal box swinging in on a web line, and its Mexico account ran a bilingual campaign encouraging fans not to miss the film in theaters.

The exact contents of the promotion are not identical everywhere. Instead, each McDonalds market appears to be building its own version of the tie in around local Happy Meal formats, which is fairly normal for a global Marvel campaign of this size.

Quick answer: McDonalds Spiderman Brand New Day is a confirmed, official cross promotion tied to the movie's July 31, 2026 release. It includes Happy Meal collectibles and printed activity content, with specifics that differ by country.

The United States rollout

In the United States, the McDonalds tie in has centered on a line of Spiderman branded bag clips featuring the character's mask, costume, and spider logo, paired with a themed activity book that accompanies Happy Meal orders. This is a lighter format than a full action figure line, closer in spirit to some of the chain's smaller scale Marvel collaborations from recent years than to a twelve piece toy set.

Marketing for the partnership leaned into the same web slinging visual used across McDonalds' international social media accounts, reinforcing the promotion as part of a coordinated global rollout rather than a one off regional idea.

The Singapore toy lineup and schedule

Singapore's version of the promotion is considerably more elaborate, and it gives a useful preview of how other markets may expand their own rollouts closer to the film's release. From July 9 through August 12, 2026, every Happy Meal purchase comes with a choice between a collectible Spiderman backpack toy or a Spiderman Brand New Day Readers book, with a fresh toy design dropping every Thursday at 11am.

Availability depends on individual restaurant stock, so calling ahead is worth doing if you are chasing a specific week's design.

Other brands running Spiderman tie ins

McDonalds is far from alone in this campaign. Little Caesars introduced a Webberoni Pizza topped with a shredded pepperoni web pattern, priced at 8.99 dollars for a large. Slim Chickens in the United Kingdom launched a themed bundle meal priced at 14.95 pounds, complete with a sweepstakes for a trip to New York City. Regional soft drink brands including Dr Pepper Mexico, Oasis in France, and Poppi have all released Spiderman themed can designs, while Baskin Robbins Australia built an entire ice cream menu around the film's red and blue color scheme.

Seeing this many brands move at once is a signal of how much marketing weight Sony and Marvel are putting behind Brand New Day, positioned as the MCU's first theatrical release since The Fantastic Four First Steps landed in 2025.

Why McDonalds keeps doing Marvel promotions

Happy Meal tie ins are not new for Spiderman. McDonalds ran promotions for The Amazing Spiderman 2 back in 2014 and for The Spectacular Spiderman animated series in 2009, so Brand New Day continues a partnership pattern that goes back well over a decade. For McDonalds, these campaigns drive family visits during a film's crucial opening window. For Sony and Marvel, brand tie ins like this one also help offset the enormous production budgets that come with a modern MCU release, which is part of why real world companies frequently show up inside the films themselves as well as in the marketing around them.

A note for collectors

Beyond families picking up Happy Meals for kids, this line is already drawing attention from adult toy collectors. Unboxing videos comparing the new backpack toys and bag clips to older Spiderman era McDonalds promotions, including the Amazing Spiderman 2 line, started appearing well ahead of the film's release. If collecting the full set matters to you, treat it the way serious collectors treat any limited run Happy Meal promotion: check in weekly, since designs rotate and stock is not guaranteed to last the full run in every restaurant.

Frequently asked questions

Is McDonalds really doing a Spiderman Brand New Day promotion?

Yes. McDonalds is one of about a dozen brands officially partnered with Sony Pictures for the Spiderman Brand New Day marketing push, alongside chains like Little Caesars and Slim Chickens.

What comes in the Spiderman Brand New Day Happy Meal?

It depends on the country. In the United States, the promotion centers on Spiderman branded bag clips and a themed activity book. In markets such as Singapore, kids can choose between a weekly rotating collectible backpack toy or a Spiderman Brand New Day Readers book.

How long does the promotion run?

Timing varies by country. Singapore's rollout runs across five weeks from July 9 to August 12, 2026, with a new toy design released every Thursday. Other regions are timing their own promotions around the movie's July 31, 2026 theatrical release.

Can adults collect the toys too?

Yes, and many are. Happy Meal collectibles tied to Marvel films have a strong adult collector following, and toy reviewers have already been unboxing and comparing this line to earlier Spiderman era McDonalds promotions.

Does the toy come with anything digital?

In at least one regional rollout, yes. Singapore's promotion pairs each physical toy with a Spiderman digital experience accessed separately from the toy itself.
