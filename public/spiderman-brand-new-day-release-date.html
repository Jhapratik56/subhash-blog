The official release date

Spiderman Brand New Day opens in United States theaters on Friday, July 31, 2026. Sony Pictures Releasing is distributing the film worldwide, and it is being counted as the 38th entry in the Marvel Cinematic Universe and the fourth film in the Tom Holland Spiderman series, following Spiderman No Way Home.

The date was not always set in stone. The film was originally slated for July 24, but the studio pushed it back a week so it would open two full weeks after Universal's The Odyssey rather than one. That gap matters because The Odyssey has an exclusive IMAX run through late July, and Sony wanted to avoid a head to head fight over premium screens.

Quick answer: Wide release is July 31, 2026. Thursday preview screenings begin the night before, on July 30, and Amazon Prime members can catch the film even earlier, on July 29.

Early screening options

You do not have to wait until July 31 if you want to be one of the first to see it. There are two earlier windows worth knowing about.

  • July 29, Wednesday: A Prime member exclusive early access screening. Amazon Prime subscribers in the United States can buy tickets through Fandango or Amazon to see the film two full days before everyone else.
  • July 30, Thursday night: Traditional preview screenings at most major chains, including AMC, Regal, and independent theaters, ahead of the Friday wide release.

Some AMC locations are also running a Dolby Cinema preview on the night of July 30 with a small collectible giveaway while supplies last, so it is worth checking your local theater's listings if you want the full premium experience on opening night.

Why Sony picked this exact weekend

Release dates for tentpole movies are rarely random, and this one is a good example. Disney had originally penciled in an unnamed Marvel Studios film for the same July slot Spiderman first targeted, but that title was pulled from the schedule in August 2024, which freed the weekend up for Sony. From there, the date shifted once more to create breathing room around The Odyssey's exclusive large format run, landing on the current July 31 date.

The result is a summer calendar where Spiderman Brand New Day is not fighting another blockbuster for the same screens, which is a big part of why advance ticket sales have already been described as the strongest opening day presales for any film in five years, according to Deadline.

What the movie is about

Following the memory erasing spell at the end of Spiderman No Way Home, the world has completely forgotten Peter Parker exists. Four years later, Peter is living alone, still protecting New York full time as Spiderman even though nobody in the city knows his name anymore. The pressure of that isolation, combined with watching his old friends move on without him, triggers a physical change in Peter that he cannot fully control, right as a new and largely invisible threat starts targeting the people he loves.

Director Destin Daniel Cretton, who previously directed Shang Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings, has described the film as leaning into a detective style mystery, built around tracking down a villain that even Spiderman himself struggles to see coming.

Where to watch it: formats and premium screens

One detail that catches a lot of people off guard is that Spiderman Brand New Day will not be playing in IMAX at launch. The Odyssey has locked up an exclusive IMAX run that carries through the Spiderman opening weekend, so Sony built its premium format plan around alternatives instead.

  • Shot for ScreenX: The first film specifically shot for CJ 4DPlex's ScreenX format, with select sequences expanding across the side walls of the auditorium for a wraparound view.
  • Dolby Cinema, RealD 3D, D Box, and 4DX are all confirmed formats at participating theaters.
  • Selected international markets will still get an IMAX release, even though the domestic run will not.
  • The film is also being finished in two separate aspect ratios, a flat 1.90 to 1 version and a wider 2.39 to 1 version, so it fills the screen fully no matter which auditorium you are sitting in.

How to get tickets

Tickets are already on sale through Fandango, AMC, Regal, and most major theater chains. If you are an Amazon Prime member, tickets for the July 29 early access screening are available directly through Amazon and Fandango, and Prime also ran a limited sweepstakes for premiere tickets earlier in the summer.

Given the early presale numbers already being described as the strongest in years, opening weekend showtimes in premium formats are likely to sell out first, so booking a few days ahead is a reasonable move if you have a specific format or seat in mind.
